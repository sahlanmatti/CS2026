Key areas of research

  • Insufficiency of traditional penetration testing frameworks regarding data protection against deep learning model security.
  • Different ways to attack against deep learning models
  • Analyzing different methods of protection strengthening information security

What's widely agreed upon?

  • Standardized testing methods are insufficient in testing unique vulnerabilities of AI models
  • There is no single method yet to protect against threats to AI models
  • Development of new protection methods is promising, but slow and challenging because of for example rapid development of attacks

What are the open questions?

  • How to develop fully automated and regulated penetration testing frameworks specially for AI models?
  • How to keep those frameworks updated to keep up with upcoming threats?

C) Threat modeling quickrun

Totally made up case, small local bank, let’s say Southia Bank Oy.

  • Ground jewels: Own and customers moneys
  • Data: Customer information (identity information, account information…)
  • Reputation: There is no bank without trust
  • Business continuity: Online banking and card payments cannot be stopped.

Bad guys:

  • Who: International cyber criminal group specialized in financial crime. Chasing money.
  • How: Ransomware, email or deep fake scam.
  • Why: Small bank, smaller Cyber Security budget

Risk 1:

    Deep fake or email scam. Criminals try to convince bank employees to send money with the wrong identity.

  • Likelihood: 20% (Within a year)
  • Impact: 500 000 € (direct financial loss)
  • Value: 20% x 500 000 € = 100 000 €

Risk 2:

    Ransomware on systems, Online banking stopped

  • Likelihood: 5% (Within a year)
  • Impact: 10 000 000 €
  • Value: 5% x 10 000 000 € = 500 000 €

Biggest risk: Ransomware paralyzing continuity / 500 000 €

What shall we do (META):

  • Mitigate, we cannot fully eliminate the risk, cyber criminals don’t stop. We cannot accept because consequences are too high, and we cannot transfer the risk to anyone else, reputation and fines are ours.
  • We are mitigating the risk by isolating main banking functions to a separate environment. Office networks are separated from having direct functionality to that environment and personnel access rights are limited to a minimum. To ensure continuity of services, we introduce unchangeable backups.
